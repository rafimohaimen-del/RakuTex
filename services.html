WORK MODEL

From inquiry to final documents, in four phases.

Select a phase to see what happens inside it.

01 🔍

Quotation & product development

Your inquiry is evaluated against what Bangladesh does best. We select the right supplier from our network, cost the product and plan development.

1 Inquiry received & specifications studied
2 Evaluation of your technical & delivery needs
3 Supplier selection & factory capacity verification
4 Detailed costing & quotation submitted
02 ⚙️

Risk analysis

We run a comprehensive SWOT analysis across yarns, dyes, manufacturing bottlenecks and buyer lead times to avert delays before cutting.

1 Fabric & yarn lead time vulnerability analysis
2 Technical pattern risk assessment
3 Production floor capacity booking validation
4 Contingency plan formulated with management
03 🛡️

Quality management

Supplier aligned and briefed, Time-and-Action (TNA) calendar fixed, quality checkpoints enforced, and testing standards confirmed.

1 Pre-production meeting with factory tech team
2 Raw material testing (shrinkage, color fastness, GSM)
3 Inline quality inspection & needle detection protocol
4 Pre-final audit and sample sign-off
04 🚢

Product execution

Final testing, packed carton inspection, vessel booking, and seamless export documentation delivery.
