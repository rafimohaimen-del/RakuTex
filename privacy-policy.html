Privacy Policy & Client Data Protection

How Raku Tex BD. protects buyer tech packs, proprietary designs, commercial information, and corporate data.

1. Client Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure (NDA)

At Raku Tex BD., we treat all tech packs, garment patterns, size charts, CADs, artwork, and commercial target prices with strict confidentiality. Proprietary files shared through our RFQ form or email are exclusively accessible to our management team and the audited manufacturing facility shortlisted for your order. We routinely execute bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to detailed sampling.

2. Factory Identity Protection

In line with standard B2B buying house practices, detailed factory addresses, audit certificates, and proprietary technical capabilities are shared with qualified buyers under mutual confidentiality to protect contractual integrity and supplier relationships.

3. Data Collection and Usage

When you complete an inquiry or request a quote, we collect your name, business email, company name, phone/WhatsApp number, country, and product requirements. This information is utilized solely to provide commercial quotations, technical feedback, sample coordination, and delivery scheduling. We never sell or distribute your contact details to third-party marketing services.

4. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions regarding our data protection policies or wish to sign a formal NDA, please contact our CEO directly at zakaria@rakutex.com.
