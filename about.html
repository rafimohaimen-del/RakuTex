ABOUT RAKU TEX BD.

More than a sourcing partner — an extension of your business.

Raku Tex BD. was established on 2 June 2024 with a clear vision: to create value and spread happiness and comfort by giving buyers reliable, sustainable and responsible apparel sourcing from Bangladesh.

OUR STORY

A new company with two decades behind it.

We are a professional garments buying house specialising in apparel sourcing, product development, quality assurance and export management. Our mission is to bridge the gap between international buyers and reliable manufacturers, with high-quality apparel, competitive pricing and dependable service.

We work closely with an experienced network of manufacturers so every product meets your specification, quality standard and delivery date. Our commitment to transparency, efficiency and long-term relationships is how we build lasting partnerships.

Leveraging our industry expertise, we deliver everyday essentials that combine quality, compliance and sustainability — built on trust and mutual success.
