SUSTAINABILITY & COMPLIANCE

The best price — never at the expense of people or planet.

Our research and development team works to bridge the gap between suppliers and retailers who believe in a sustainable future, making sure materials are sourced ethically and the workers behind every garment are treated and paid fairly.

WHY IT MATTERS

Buyers want to know where their clothes come from.

Sustainability means keeping the environment at a level the next generation can live with. The apparel industry is a major contributor to emissions and water waste, and some raw materials depend on fertilisers and harmful substances.

We believe responsible, eco-friendly manufacturing — with organic sourcing, recycling and fair wages — can make RMG a sustainability leader. Meeting these criteria is now how suppliers compete and survive.

CERTIFICATION DIRECTORY

Standards held across our partner
factories.

Certificates belong to individual factories, not to Raku Tex. Valid copies for your order are shared on request.

GOTS Materials

Global Organic Textile Standard — organic fibres with environmental and social criteria through processing.

OCS Materials

Organic Content Standard — verifies the organic material content of a product.

GRS Materials

Global Recycled Standard — recycled content plus social, environmental and chemical requirements.

RCS Materials

Recycled Claim Standard — verifies recycled material content through the supply chain.

Better Cotton Materials

Cotton grown under the Better Cotton programme for more sustainable farming.

OEKO-TEX Materials

STANDARD 100 — textiles tested for harmful substances.

Fairtrade Social

Fair terms of trade for cotton farmers and textile workers.

amfori BSCI Social

Social-compliance auditing of working conditions in the supply chain.

SEDEX / SMETA Social

Ethical trade audits shared through the Sedex platform.

WRAP Social

Certification for lawful, humane and ethical manufacturing.

ICS Social

Initiative for Compliance and Sustainability — retailer-led social audits.

Higg FEM Environment

Higg Facility Environmental Module — measures a facility's environmental performance.

bluesign Environment

Chemical management and resource efficiency in textile production.

Energy management systems.
